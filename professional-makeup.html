Micro-Fine Formulation

High-Definition (HD) Makeup

With the prevalence of high-definition 4K video cameras and studio strobe flashes, standard makeup can occasionally appear thick, chalky, or reveal texture.

Our HD makeup formulations employ micro-fine spherical pigments that refract and scatter light evenly across the face. This creates an optical soft-focus veil, minimizing the appearance of uneven pores while preserving natural skin luminescence without heavy white flash-back.

Ideal For: High-resolution wedding videos, anchor telecasts, corporate portraits, and close-up photography.

The Professional Application Process

Engineered to produce clean, camera-ready results that honor your natural facial features and skin health.

01

Lighting & Concept Check

Reviewing camera setups, daylight vs. flash lighting, wardrobe palette, and director references.

02

Pore Refinement Prep

Targeted oil balancing or deep hydration priming to achieve a smooth, flash-compliant base.

03

Micro-Pigment Layering

Application of HD or airbrush base with precision spot concealing rather than heavy blanketing.

04

Test Shot & Calibration

Quick digital camera flash check to inspect blending, symmetry, and glare before the live session.
